Friday
The Vision & Roadmap

Your wife is thinking about more things than you are. Friday fixes that.

Friday is a personal life operating system — one home base for everything that actually matters, so the instinct becomes "check Friday," not "check Instagram." It's built as a team of specialists, each one quietly expert in one part of your life, reporting up to a single coordinator who tells you what to actually do about it.

Why One Agent Isn't Enough
A house inspector can't tell you about your marriage

Every "AI assistant" that tries to do everything ends up generic at everything. Friday's bet is the opposite: nine specialists, each with real domain expertise, each watching one part of your life closely enough to say something you didn't already know — then one coordinator who holds all of it at once, because you shouldn't have to.

01
Specialists find what generalists miss
A general assistant knows a little about your house. House Manager knows your house specifically — your oil tank, your panel brand, your inspection report from fourteen months ago that nobody ever went back to.

The coordinator is where the real value lives

Not Hypothetical
This already works — here's actual output

No invented demo data. These are real findings from the two agents built so far, running against real answers.

House Manager
Your oil tank is old, its gauge is broken, you don't have a delivery provider on file, and heating season is weeks away. Each fact alone is nothing. Together: you could run dry mid-winter without warning.
Relationship Manager
Three of your closest friends, described separately, in depth — and all three got the same unprompted answer: "I don't see him enough." That pattern only exists if you hold all three answers together. No single conversation would have surfaced it.
Personal Entertainment Manager
Asked what's on the watchlist, the honest answer was "I don't know what to watch, which is why I'm here." No invented recommendation — the real fix was noticing there's no backlog at all, and handing back the one thing that already works every time: the audiobook you already reach for when nothing else gets picked.
The Team
Nine specialists, one coordinator

Reprioritized 2026-10-07 around a sharper target user — home, food, and daily entertainment first, since those are the three things that touch every day. Built one at a time, each one proven against real use before the next one starts.

1. House Manager
Live & tested
Service providers, maintenance, safety — the things a new homeowner doesn't know they don't know.
2. Personal Entertainment Manager
Live & tested
What to watch, read, or listen to tonight — decided before the free hour starts, not during it.
3. Food Manager
Building now
Cooking, leftovers, the freezer, delivery, or going out — deciding which, for every night of the week, plus the daily kid-meal logistics.
4. Relationship Manager
Live & tested
Who matters, how the connection is doing, what moment is coming up.
5. Goals and Tasks Manager
Planned
The bucket list and the big rocks that are easy to let slide — plus the catch-all for anything that doesn't have another natural home, like car maintenance or a security to-do.
6. Travel and Experiences Manager
Planned
Trip planning and local ideas, organized by how much time you actually have.
7. Health Manager
Planned
Fitness goals that survive a real schedule, plus the doctor and dentist appointments that are easy to let slide. Personal-level for now.
8. Family Scheduler
New, planned
Who's covering childcare, pickups and dropoffs, and what work travel or an evening event breaks — "knowing where the bodies are," and it only gets harder with more kids.
9. Restaurant Manager
Building right after Food Manager
Where to eat and the booking — split off from Food Manager since it's a genuinely different workflow. Deliberately builds depth in nearby towns that usually get overlooked, not just the default spots.

Financial Advisor and Social Coordinator remain real ideas, deliberately archived for now rather than built — the first pending a real decision about financial-advice scope, the second a true v2+ network-effect feature that needs more than one agent built first.

Where This Is Headed
Built bottom-up, on purpose

Each specialist gets built and proven on its own before the coordinator that links them gets built at all — synthesis can't be designed until there's real output to synthesize.

House Manager, Personal Entertainment Manager & Relationship Manager — built and validated
All three run against real data and produce findings nobody had to invent.
Food Manager, then Restaurant Manager
The other most asked-about decision in a given week — what to eat, plus the daily kid-meal logistics — then where to actually go, split into its own agent since booking and occasion-matching turned out to be a different job entirely.
Goals and Tasks, Travel and Experiences, Health — then Family Scheduler
A new addition, 2026-10-07: a dedicated agent for who's covering childcare, pickups/dropoffs, and the conflicts travel creates — a problem that gets exponentially harder as more kids enter the picture, not easier.
Friday, the coordinator
Built last, on purpose — the thing that reads every specialist's findings and tells you the one thing that actually matters today.

This page describes where Friday is actually headed, not where it is today — every "planned" item above is exactly that, planned, not built. Built privately. Not yet public, not yet for sale.
